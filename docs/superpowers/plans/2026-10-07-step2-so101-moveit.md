# Step 2: SO-101 + ros2_control + MoveIt Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** executed inline (superpowers:executing-plans). Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** Replace the step 1 arm with a simulated SO-101 that grasps with its own jaws, driven by ros2_control and MoveIt, and re-run the benchmark against the step 1 baseline.

**Architecture:** Three ROS packages: `so101_description` (model), `so101_moveit_config` (planning) and `lab_simulation` (world, tasks, benchmark). The robot is spawned into Gazebo from `robot_description`; `move_group` plans joint-space goals produced by a NumPy IK; tasks stay pure Python behind small adapter interfaces.

**Tech Stack:** ROS 2 Jazzy, Gazebo Harmonic, gz_ros2_control, MoveIt 2 (OMPL, pick_ik), NumPy, pytest.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-07-step2-so101-moveit-design.md`

## Review Focus

1. Grasp gate: a jaw that closes on nothing, or on the wrong piece, must never latch.
2. A planning failure must fail the trial, never hang or abort the run.
3. Uncapped simulation speed: every command that must take effect before the next step waits for an acknowledgement (latch state, gripper settle, action result), never a fixed delay.
4. Comparisons across robots or grasp modes must carry a warning.
5. Scene reset with a piece still latched or squeezed: release before teleporting.
